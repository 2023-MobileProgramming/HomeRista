# HomeRista
An Android app that helps you brew the perfect cup of coffee.

## Purpose
This project was a team project for the Mobile Programming class during the winter semester of 2023. The theme of the project was commercial applications, so our team chose coffee because the coffee market is growing every year. We specifically focused on people who brew coffee at home.

Many home brewers are passionate about making their coffee taste even better, and this app is designed to help them brew more delicious coffee.

### Key Features
* Record your coffee brewing process.
* Save information about your coffee beans.
* Review saved brewing records.
* Discover diverse coffee-related content.
* Shop for coffee beans and brewing tools.
